# CANON — the iron laws of DYNAMIS (v2)

Laws 1–15 are inherited from THE WEAVE charter (`Zip/NETWORK-EVOLUTION.md`, Sept 2026),
each paid for by a measured failure of the estate or of Steem/Hive/Blurt.
Laws 16–21 are new in this canon version, paid for by the 2026 estate audit and the
frontier scan of 2026-11. **The canon wins over every other document.**

## Inherited laws (compressed)

1. **Custody comes first.** No code touches a network before its keys pass the census
   (2026-10-21 key-rotation incident: 12 accounts stranded in one raidable sandbox).
2. **Two-soul intake.** A key exists only after it is in two independent stores — the
   sealed device vault and the cloud vault — with the chain-judged census as the roster.
3. **HSM-only signing.** Keys never travel in plaintext; signatures happen inside the HSM
   boundary; the census is the single roster of the living.
4. **Measured, or it did not happen.** Receipts first, consensus second, and no key runs
   without fingerprint law. A number that was not measured is not published.
5. **Consensus-verifiable capacity.** The pool advertises exactly its census-verified,
   token-pages-publishable capacity.
6. **"Soon" never appears in a metric.**
7. **Persistence.** Sandboxes die (the fleet survived four wipes in one month); state is
   re-establishable from sealed ledgers; every component boots itself from the chain,
   not from a machine.
8. **Honest budget.** This plan itself: capacity, income, membership figures quoted in
   chapter 12 are targets, and the first dashboard shows the gap between target and
   measured without embarrassment.
9. **Fraud economics above tolerated fraud.** The verification band's budget must always
   exceed the maximum profitable fraud it can catch.
10. **Government limits.** DYN is a work-and-utility instrument with no investment
    promise; the network publishes no yield marketing.
11. **Fuel rails stay small and competent.** Any regulated-market activity on fuel rails
    stays at the fleet's existing, small scale until competent advice says otherwise.
12. **The book-keeping rule.** This plan itself: capacity, income, membership quoted are
    targets until measured.
13. **The army is a paid job.** No account posts without chain-verified custody and a
    drafted, budgeted work order (army-social REFUSED-NO-CUSTODY).
14. **Anti-herd by construction.** Social schedules are seeded (`mulberry32(day|account)`),
    reproducible post-hoc, capped, and gapped — the herd pattern is a Sybil signature.
15. **Witnesses are the next generation** of the estate's deterministic derivative law:
    consensus duties derive from measured custody, never from self-declaration.

## New laws (16–21)

16. **Root sovereignty.** The private network is the root. Books, custody, and business
    logic never leave the root. Public networks (DYNAMIS, Steem, Hive, any chain) are
    utilities it consumes — never the system of record for its existence.
17. **Representation separation.** Every presence on a public network is a client persona
    with its own scoped keys and its own context. A persona never claims to represent the
    root, another persona, or an entity, unless its charter explicitly says so. Blast
    radius is per-persona: the compromise of one burns one, never the fleet.
18. **Capability, not credential.** Every integration — internal or external — receives
    the least capability that works: scoped, time-boxed, revocable tokens. Dependencies
    are one-way (root → network → rail). A dependency that must call back into the root
    is designed out, not worked around.
19. **Business before token.** No emission, incentive, or market may precede a measured
    business flow — a job paid by a real entity budget for a real deliverable. Emission
    follows revenue; it never leads it. (This is the anti-Steem law: Steem led with
    inflation and spent a decade defending it.)
20. **The org is a contract.** A business entity exists in the network only with: a
    charter, a delegation-of-authority matrix, budget envelopes, a KPI tree, and a named
    human regulator with veto keys. An agent crew without all five is a sandbox toy, not
    an entity.
21. **Exit always.** Any external dependency — chain, rail, model provider, marketplace —
    must be removable within one working day without loss of books, custody, or identity.
    Every integration ships with its exit runbook on the day it ships.
